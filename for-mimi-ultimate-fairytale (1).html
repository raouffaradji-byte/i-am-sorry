To my dearest Meriem, My Queen,

I wish you could see yourself through my eyes, even if it was just for a moment. Maybe then you’d understand why I look at you with so much love, why your smile can make my entire day better, and why even after we’ve just finished talking, I somehow find myself missing you again.

I don’t think you realise how much you mean to me. You’re not just someone I love. You’re my favourite person, my comfort, my happiness, and the girl I want beside me through everything.

I love everything about you, Meriem. Your beautiful smile, your eyes, your laugh, your little expressions, the way you care, and even the smallest things you probably don’t notice about yourself. I love how you always give your best, how you know exactly how to reach my heart, and how you manage to make me feel safe, warm, and loved without even trying.

And please, stop doubting your beauty. Seriously, you need glasses if you can’t see it. If beauty could talk, it would probably say, “God made me beautiful as her.” And I would still be standing there agreeing with it.

You came into my life without promising to change anything, yet somehow you changed so much. You made me start believing in myself again. Things I once thought were impossible started feeling possible. You gave me reasons to work harder, take care of myself, make plans, and look forward to tomorrow. I started seeing colours in places where I used to see nothing.

And the best part is that you never had to ask me to become a better person. Your presence alone made me want to.

I want you to know that you never have to be a certain version of yourself for me to love you. You can be happy, sad, tired, quiet, stubborn, silly, emotional, or anything in between. I want all of you. You don’t have to hide your feelings or pretend everything is okay. I want to be there for you just as you’ve been there for me.

I know we won’t always have perfect days, but I don’t want a difficult moment to make us forget everything beautiful we have. I want us to understand each other, keep choosing each other, and never stop trying.

And when I think about the future, I don’t imagine something complicated. I imagine you and me. Holding your hand, hugging you tightly, making you laugh, bothering you just to see your reactions, and sharing all those ordinary little moments that would become extraordinary simply because they’re with you.

I want to marry you, Meriem. I want to build a home with you, spoil you with affection, kiss your forehead, remind you how beautiful you are, and make sure you never have to wonder whether you’re loved.

I promise you my honesty, my loyalty, and my whole heart. I promise to keep showing you how much I love you, not just through words, but through the little things I do every day. And I promise that my love won’t stay the same or become less. It will keep growing, and I’ll keep finding new reasons to fall in love with you.

You’re my one and only, my most and best, my cute and adorable girl, my love and my dear Meriem. You’re the girl I’ll spend my whole life loving.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for coming into my life. And thank you for making this world feel so much more beautiful.

If I were given a thousand lifetimes, I’d spend every single one finding you, choosing you, and loving you all over again.

Forever yours,
Raouf
